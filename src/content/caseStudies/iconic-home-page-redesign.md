## Background

The app homepage was outdated and engagement was decreasing year on year. Based on previous research and customer feedback, this was due to a lack of personalised and relevant content. An external usability audit also flagged concerns, including that the homepage felt too ad-heavy.

![The existing app homepage experience](/case-studies/app%20homepage/App%20Home%201%20-%20intro.png)

![Current state across marketing and promotional content](/case-studies/app%20homepage/App%20Home%202%20-%20background.png)

## Approach

I reviewed existing resources to identify gaps and opportunities - including a Baymard usability audit, NPS feedback, and UserVoice suggestions. Through this, I identified key pain points to solve for: improving category wayfinding, reducing ad-looking content, including core utility functions, providing discovery and inspiration, and tailoring content to individual customers.

![Key customer problems and competitor analysis](/case-studies/app%20homepage/App%20Home%203%20-%20discovery.png)

I ran stakeholder sessions to walk through the current state, rationale, limitations, and pain points within the existing CMS marketing modules, and conducted a competitor analysis across 20+ ecommerce apps.

I went through many rounds of iteration, testing, design critiques, and stakeholder feedback to refine the designs from wires through to high fidelity. I collaborated closely with the Creative Team to stress test marketing modules and identify where they may break.

![Design iteration, module breakdown, and handover documentation](/case-studies/app%20homepage/App%20Home%204%20-%20design.png)

Overall I designed 16 modules including refreshing existing marketing modules and adding new personalised content, with detailed documentation to support development. I also created a 60-slide handover document for the marketing team.

## Outcome

Initial A/B testing of the new personalised modules resulted in a 22% increase in engagement with homepage content. These modules included recommendations based on wishlist, new arrivals from favourite brands, wishlist alerts, recently viewed, and more.
