## Background

I was part of the team engaged for Phase 1 of Vodafone Hutchison Australia's (VHA) Digital Transformation journey. This first phase focused on uplifting the current website (vodafone.com.au) and online store (shop.vodafone.com.au).

I was a hands-on lead, managing a team of 5 UX and UI designers, as well as indirectly supporting 2 Service Designers and 4 BAs. I did this alongside delivering detailed design work for the client.

## Approach

During the initial Elaboration phase, I led a team to analyse and understand the current state of Vodafone's experience across their products. Working with stakeholders across the company, we delved into each customer journey to identify their goals, needs, and pain points - conducting new research and reviewing previous research, all of which informed our detailed design.

As part of this project, I also played a critical role in creating a design system to set up Vodafone for continued success. This included defining refreshed brand guidelines, creating a component library, developing content guidelines, and establishing UX rationale and guidelines.

Throughout the project, I engaged with various stakeholders, including business, technical, and legal teams, to ensure alignment and drive engagement towards shared goals.

## Outcome

My team delivered an all new and refreshed customer experience, brand, and UI. In addition, we handed over a design system for the Vodafone team to use going forward, simplifying and streamlining their design process.
