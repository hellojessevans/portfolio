## Background

As part of reducing marketing costs, THE ICONIC wanted to better leverage and grow owned channels (email, SMS, app push), improving both how we talk to customers and how we use those channels. Customers who follow their favourite brands visit more often and are more likely to convert, so increasing follow-the-brand engagement was a key lever.

![The existing brand-following experience across web and mobile](/case-studies/favourite%20brands/Favourite%20brands%202%20-%20background.png)

## Approach

From existing quant and qual data, I identified 3 key customer problems: the brand catalogue felt bloated and stale, customers didn't feel like we were personalising their experience despite knowing who they are, and some brands were unfamiliar and hard to evaluate.

I kicked off discovery with initial data analysis and mapped current-state experience journeys. I ran an Opportunity Workshop with stakeholders to explore the problem space, gain shared understanding of key problems, and identify where the opportunities and gaps were. This helped shape the research.

![Research process and opportunity workshop](/case-studies/favourite%20brands/Favourite%20brands%203-%20discovery.png)

I ran moderated, generative, behavioural research sessions to understand how customers interact with brands, their expectations around the follow-the-brand feature, and what they would expect in a customer profile. Key insights included that customers didn't see value in the current feature and many didn't notice it at all.

I prioritised two short-term initiatives: delivering a newness email and push messages using current brand data, and refreshing the visibility, consistency and messaging around the follow-the-brand feature across all web and app touchpoints.

![Design exploration, affinity mapping, and user flows](/case-studies/favourite%20brands/Favourite%20brands%204%20-%20design.png)

## Outcome

Refreshing the existing follow-the-brand feature increased Active Customers who favourite brands by 5% (from 25% to 30%). Since sales per customer are ~27% higher after they favourite a brand, this generated approximately $200k in additional sales.

The product page became the main page customers interacted with brands, and within the first month web brand-favouriting had doubled compared to the previous year (up 100.9%). iOS favouriting increased by 67.4% over the same period.
