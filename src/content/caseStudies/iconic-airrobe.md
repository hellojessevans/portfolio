## Background

THE ICONIC partnered with AirRobe, an Australian-founded second-hand fashion marketplace, providing an integration that allowed customers to add purchases to AirRobe to rent, resell or recycle. Average Basket Value and Average Basket Size were higher for AirRobe customers compared to average.

![THE ICONIC x AirRobe partnership announcement](/case-studies/airrobe/Airrobe%201%20-%20background.png)

## Approach

Despite allowing customers to add products to AirRobe during purchase, a key part of the experience was missing: the ability to add past purchases. I ran mixed-method research sessions including generative research to explore behaviours and motivations around buying and selling second-hand items, usability testing of the existing pre-purchase experience, and concept testing for the reselling historical orders flow.

![Research insights and synthesis](/case-studies/airrobe/Airrobe%202%20-%20discovery.png)

Key insights: customers were unsure what AirRobe was and whether it was part of THE ICONIC or a third party, people were satisfied with their existing resale platforms, and the main motivator for reselling was to recoup money and keep their wardrobe fresh - sustainability was not a primary driver.

I designed a new resale section within the account, allowing customers to easily view all previous eligible purchases. A key learning was that differentiating between AirRobe and THE ICONIC was confusing, so by integrating management functions into our experience, it felt like an ICONIC-owned feature rather than a third party. I also integrated links to the resale page within the Returns and Order details experiences.

![Re-sell items flow across account, selection, and listing screens](/case-studies/airrobe/Airrobe%203%20-%20design.png)

## Outcome

Within the first few days of launch (July 2022), with no marketing or comms, 6,633 unique customers landed on the new resell page and 1,140 items were sent to AirRobe. Within 6 weeks, this grew to 69,143 unique visits and 38,207 items listed.

After almost a year live, orders added to AirRobe increased from ~3.3k to ~15-19k monthly, items increased from 9k to 67k monthly average, Average Basket Value increased 60% higher vs baseline, and live listings grew from 1.5-2k to 16-20k each month.
