## Background

I was the Experience Lead on a team engaged by the Digital Transformation Agency (DTA) to design and build a proof of concept for the future of digital interactions with government. The challenge was to un-complicate the way people find, understand, and access information across various government agencies.

Just before the end of the project, COVID-19 hit. We pivoted and built the Australia.gov.au site (responsible for all daily updates and restriction rules), with the Prime Minister and Cabinet's Office wanting us to launch the myGov POC designs as a live Beta.

## Approach

I began the design process with sketches, paper and white-boarding sessions, working with the team to conduct user research, create journey maps and high-level flows and features. Working alongside content designers, I adopted a content-led design approach to ideate and create a new content model that supported the extensive catalog of government information.

The new experience structured information around key life events, aligning with users' perception of the government as a single entity rather than separate agencies. We also re-designed the logged-in experience, creating a single platform to view information, benefits, and services without the need to understand complex governmental structures.

## Outcome

During the myGov Beta phase, my team successfully implemented over 70 features, 400 user stories, and 270 design variations, with our primary focus being on services impacted by COVID-19.

![The future of myGov - live Beta](/case-studies/myGov/myGov.png)

The myGov authenticated experience empowers users to self-service, providing a personalised and guided journey that streamlines the process of discovering and applying for payments. The success of this project has laid the groundwork for scaling the solution across agencies and future releases, showcasing the potential for a whole-of-government approach.
